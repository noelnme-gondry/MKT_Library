## In one line

A lookalike audience is the platform finding people who resemble a seed list you provide. What you put in the seed determines what comes out.

## The seed is the whole game

The platform learns the shared characteristics of your seed and finds people like them. Seed it with "people who click ads" and that is what you get back — which can be a different population from people who spend.

- Good seed: users who purchased repeatedly or rank in top [LTV](/glossary/ltv)
- Risky seed: users who only installed or only signed up

This is a frequent explanation for cheap CPI alongside below-average LTV.

## Size matters too

Too small and no pattern emerges; too large and your users' distinctiveness washes out. If narrowing to top-LTV users underperforms, suspect seed volume before seed quality.
