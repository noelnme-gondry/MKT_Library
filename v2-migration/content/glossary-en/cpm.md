## In one line

**CPM (Cost Per Mille, "mille" = thousand)** is the cost per 1,000 impressions — the closest thing to the raw media price a platform charges.

## Why it matters

When CPM rises, it usually means the auction got more competitive (seasonality, competitors upping bids) or your targeting got more expensive. Since [CPC](/glossary/cpc), [CPI](/glossary/cpi), and [CPA](/glossary/cpa) are all downstream of CPM through CTR and CVR, tracking CPM on its own helps isolate one specific cause when costs rise.
