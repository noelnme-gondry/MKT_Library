## In one line

Frequency is how many times the average person saw the same ad: impressions ÷ reach.

## It says little on its own

Frequency alone rarely tells you good or bad. Its value comes from pairing.

- Frequency ↑ with CTR ↓ → likely [creative fatigue](/glossary/creative-fatigue)
- Frequency ↑ with CPM ↑ → still bidding into the same narrow pool
- Frequency flat with CTR ↓ → the cause is outside the creative

## Where it matters most

Campaigns with small audiences, [retargeting](/glossary/retargeting) above all. A small pool pushes frequency up quickly, and without a cap you keep paying more to reach the same people.
