## In one line

Show the same people the same creative long enough and response cools. Frequency climbing while CTR falls — that scissor shape — is the fatigue signal.

## Separating it from other causes

A falling CTR makes you want to swap creative immediately. Check frequency first.

- Frequency ↑ with CTR ↓ → fatigue is likely. Rotate creative or widen the audience.
- Frequency flat with CTR ↓ → probably not fatigue. Look at competition, season, targeting changes. New creative will not fix this one.

A drop in blended CTR is also frequently just a larger mix of new assets still in learning.

## When rotating

Swapping every underperformer at once destabilises [learning](/glossary/learning-phase). Replace the bottom few instead.
