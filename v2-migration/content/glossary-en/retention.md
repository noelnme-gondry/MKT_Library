## In one line

**Retention** is the share of users who installed or signed up at a given point that are still coming back some days later — commonly measured as D1, D7, or D30.

## Why it matters

No matter how much new traffic comes in, if it all leaks out, you're "pouring water into a leaking bucket." Retention shows whether a product actually holds on to people. It's best measured per [cohort](/glossary/cohort).

## A common mistake

Don't average retention across cohorts unweighted — cohorts vary in size, so a naive average overweights small cohorts. Weight by cohort size (installs/signups) for an accurate read.
