## In one line

**CPC (Cost Per Click)** is the cost of a single click — equal to **cost per impression ÷ CTR**, or `(CPM ÷ 1,000) ÷ CTR`. At a $5 CPM and a 1% CTR, CPC is $0.50. CPM is priced per 1,000 impressions, so dividing it by CTR directly inflates the result 1,000×.

## Why it matters

At the same CPM, higher [CTR](/glossary/ctr) means lower CPC. So when CPC rises, figure out whether the platform got more expensive (CPM up) or your creative stopped working (CTR down) — the fix is completely different depending on which it is.
