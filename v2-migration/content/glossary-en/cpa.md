## In one line

Spend ÷ conversions. CPA (Cost Per Action) is what one desired action cost you — a signup, a purchase.

## Why it matters

Where [CPI](/glossary/cpi) tells you the cost of getting an install, CPA tells you the cost of getting a real, valuable action. Most campaign optimization ultimately converges on lowering CPA.

## Finding the cause when CPA is high

CPA is a downstream result. The upstream metrics — CPM, CTR, CVR — usually explain why: expensive impressions, weak clicks, or poor post-click conversion.

## Run the numbers
